# Borrow and Renew Books

## Who can borrow
Patrons with a valid AUC ID card who are eligible to borrow can check out books at the circulation desk on the plaza level of the Main Library, or at a self-checkout unit on each floor. The borrowing card must be in good standing, with no fines and no overdue books.

## Loan periods and limits
| Patron type | Loan period | Maximum books |
|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate students | 28 days | 20 |
| Graduate students | Academic semester | 30 |
| Faculty and administrators | Academic semester | 30 |
| Adjunct faculty | Academic semester | 30 |
| Emeritus professors | Academic semester | 30 |
| Alumni | 14 days | 5 |

Undergraduate students can borrow up to 20 books for 28 days. Graduate students can borrow up to 30 books for an academic semester. Faculty, administrators, adjunct faculty and emeritus professors can borrow up to 30 books for an academic semester. Alumni can borrow up to 5 books for 14 days.

## Renewals
Books can be renewed on or before the due date, provided there is no hold on the book. You can renew online, in person at the circulation desk on the plaza level of the Main Library, by telephone, or by email.

# SRC Library

## Opening hours
The Social Research Center (SRC) Library is open Sunday to Thursday, from 10:00 am to 3:30 pm.

## Who can use it
The SRC Library is open for on-site use by SRC staff, AUC staff and students, and SRC visitors. Check-out privileges are limited to SRC staff, who may borrow up to 5 items for 14 days and must present a valid ID card each time. Other users cannot take materials out overnight; materials must be returned before the end of regular library hours.

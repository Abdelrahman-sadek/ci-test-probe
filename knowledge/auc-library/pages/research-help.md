# Research help

## Subject librarians
Each AUC school, and some departments, has an assigned librarian who is the first point of contact for library questions, including instruction, reference assistance, purchase requests and feedback on resources. Find your subject librarian on the library Contact Us page.

## Research consultations
You can schedule an in-person or virtual meeting with library research staff for help with a research project, finding sources or citing them.

## Special collections research help
For rare books, archives and special collections, contact the RBSCL research services staff listed on the RBSCL Contact Us page at https://library.aucegypt.edu/libraries/rbscl/contact-us.

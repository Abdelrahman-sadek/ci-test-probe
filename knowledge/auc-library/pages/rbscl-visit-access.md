# RBSCL visit and access

## Location
The visitor entrance to the Rare Books and Special Collections Library (RBSCL) is on the third floor of the Main Library building on AUC's New Cairo campus, on Bartlett Plaza at the center of campus. The third floor holds the RBSCL Research Help Desk, the John Gerhart Reading Room and study carrels. The main exhibition spaces are the Abdallah S. Jum'ah Exhibition Room and the Richard and Kerry Bartlett Exhibition Foyer.

## AUC visitors
AUC students, faculty and staff are welcome to visit the library. For use of archives or other specialized assistance, making an appointment with a staff member is recommended.

## External visitors
External visitors from outside AUC are welcome but should contact library staff first to discuss their needs, because staff must clear the visit with campus and library security. External visitors must bring a passport or a government photo ID. External users who plan to use the library extensively must apply for a user card and pay the applicable fees.

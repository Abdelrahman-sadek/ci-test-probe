# RBSCL collections

## Collection strengths
The Rare Books and Special Collections Library documents ancient, medieval and modern Egypt and the wider region, with particular strengths in Egyptology, Islamic art and architecture, and travel literature. Its holdings include rare books, the University Archives, archives and manuscripts, oral history interviews, regional architecture collections and photography collections.

## Digital library
The Rare Books and Special Collections Digital Library was established in fall 2011 to publish born-digital materials and digital copies of cultural heritage collections, including historical photographs. It runs on OCLC CONTENTdm and is openly accessible online. It can include University Archives, rare books, archives and manuscripts, oral history interviews, regional architecture and photography collections. Check the digital library first, then request reading-room access for originals.

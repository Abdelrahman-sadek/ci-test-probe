# AUC Knowledge Fountain

## What it is
AUC Knowledge Fountain (fount.aucegypt.edu) is AUC's institutional repository, run by AUC Libraries and Learning Technologies on the Digital Commons platform. It is the place to find AUC theses and dissertations, master's theses, capstone and graduation projects, and other student and faculty research.

## Finding AUC theses
Theses and dissertations are grouped by school, department and research center, for example the Social Research Center, History, Engineering and Rhetoric collections. Browse by discipline or author, or search the whole repository by keyword.

## Collections
The Rare Books and Special Collections Library holds rare books, maps, manuscripts, personal papers, and
historical photographs of Cairo and Egypt. Many photographs are digitised in the digital collections.

## Visiting and appointments
Rare books are used in the reading room only. Book an appointment by email at least two working days
in advance. Bring your AUC ID or passport. Photography permission must be requested separately.

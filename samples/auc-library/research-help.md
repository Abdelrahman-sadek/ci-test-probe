## Finding peer-reviewed articles
Start in the library discovery search and filter by "peer-reviewed". For environmental topics such as water
scarcity in Egypt, use subject databases listed in the A–Z databases page. For Arabic sources, search with
Arabic keywords and transliteration variants.

## Off-campus access
Databases are available from home after signing in with your AUC account through the library proxy.

## Citations
Librarians can help with APA 7, MLA, and Chicago citation styles. AUC theses are available in the digital repository.

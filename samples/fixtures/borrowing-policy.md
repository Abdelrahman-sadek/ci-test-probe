## Loan limits
Undergraduate students can borrow up to 15 books for 3 weeks. Graduate students can borrow up to 30 books
for one semester. Faculty can borrow up to 50 books for one semester.

## Alumni and visitors
Alumni can borrow up to 5 books with a paid alumni library card. Visitors can use books inside the library
only and cannot borrow.

## Renewals and fines
Books can be renewed online twice if no one has placed a hold. Overdue books cannot be renewed online;
return the book to the circulation desk. The overdue fine is 5 EGP per book per day.

## Interlibrary loan
If a book is not in the catalog, request it through interlibrary loan (ILL) or document delivery from the
library website. Delivery usually takes 1 to 3 weeks.

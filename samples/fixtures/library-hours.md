## Main Library hours
During the fall semester the Main Library opens Sunday to Thursday from 8:00 am and closes at 10:00 pm.
On Friday the library is closed. On Saturday it opens from 12:00 pm and closes at 8:00 pm.

## Exam period hours
During final exams the Main Library extends its hours and closes at midnight Sunday to Thursday.

## Ramadan hours
During Ramadan the library closes at 4:00 pm.
